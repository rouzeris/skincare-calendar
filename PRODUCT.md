# Product

## Register

product

(App UI = product register. `www/` landing = brand register — override per task there.)

## Users

Women who care for their skin daily — PL/UA/EN speaking, mostly mobile.
Context: bathroom shelf, morning/evening ritual, spotty attention, one hand free.
Job: know what to apply today, when products expire, keep the ritual going.

## Product Purpose

Cera = skincare routine calendar. Track morning/evening routines on a 14-day
calendar, manage product shelf with PAO/expiry, plan frequencies (daily /
specific days / interval). Local-first Expo app; Convex backend incoming
(auth, sync, shared product archive, assistant). Success: the ritual sticks —
users return twice a day without friction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, caring, ritual. Skincare as self-care, not a chore. Warm, unhurried,
trustworthy — a gentle companion, never clinical, never loud. Voice speaks
like a knowledgeable friend, not a dermatologist or an influencer.

## Anti-references

- Beauty-influencer aesthetics: glossy gradients, sparkle, "glow up" hype.
- Clinical/pharma coldness: sterile white, blue crosses, lab language.
- Generic SaaS landing grammar: hero-metric template, identical card grids,
  eyebrow-above-every-section.
- Medical claims. Assistant escalates serious problems to a doctor; the brand
  never promises treatment.

## Design Principles

1. Ritual over checklist — the interface should feel like a moment of care,
   not task management.
2. Calm hierarchy — one thing at a time; generous whitespace; no competing CTAs.
3. Trust through softness — rounded, warm, tactile; safety without sterility.
4. Identity continuity — Rose (#E11D48) + Stone neutrals carry across app and
   landing; landing may push the rose bolder.
5. Trilingual by default — PL/UA/EN are equals; layouts must survive all three.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body contrast ≥4.5:1. Reduced-motion alternatives for all
animation. Text-size resilient layouts (app already ships PL/UA/EN i18n).
